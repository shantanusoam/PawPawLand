Our Story

Born From Love,
Not Just Business {% include "website/partials/squiggle.html" %}

Our inspiration came from our own journey with our Golden Retriever puppy, who needed thoughtful supervision, emotional reassurance, and carefully managed socialisation, especially after experiencing health complications as a curious young pup.

Through that experience, we learned firsthand what modern dog parents are searching for: not just care, but comfort. Not just supervision, but understanding. Not just socialisation, but meaningful, well-matched interactions shaped around each dog's temperament and energy.
